1. Overview

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase from ShopAutoEquip.com. If a product doesn't meet your needs, we accept returns under the terms below.

This Return Policy covers returns for any reason — change of mind, wrong fit, ordering error. For products that arrived damaged in shipping or have a defect that emerged after use, see our Warranty Information page instead.

The return window begins on the date the carrier delivers the product to your shipping address (as confirmed by tracking).

3. Eligible Items

To qualify for return, items must be:

  • Within the return window stated above
  • Unused or used minimally (only as needed to determine fit/suitability)
  • In original condition — undamaged, with all original packaging, accessories, manuals, and documentation
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or order confirmation email)

4. Non-Returnable Items

The following are not eligible for return:

  • Custom-configured or special-order items built specifically for your order
  • Used or installed equipment showing wear beyond inspection use
  • Equipment with damaged or missing packaging that prevents safe restocking and resale
  • Hazardous materials subject to shipping restrictions (lubricants, refrigerants, etc.)
  • Items marked "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" on the product page
  • Items returned outside the return window

5. Condition Requirements

To receive a full refund (less restocking fee, if applicable), returned items must be:

  • In their original carton with all internal packaging materials
  • Free of damage caused by use, installation, or transit back to SAR
  • Complete with all included accessories, hardware, manuals, and documentation

Items returned in damaged or incomplete condition may be subject to additional fees, partial refund, or return refusal.

  • Items damaged in transit (replacement issued)
  • Wrong items shipped by SAR (no fee, no return shipping cost to customer)

8. How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact us first. Email [INSERT RETURNS EMAIL] or call 856.547.8700 with your order number and the reason for return. Do not ship the item back without contacting us first — we'll issue a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and confirm where to ship.
  2. Receive your RMA number. SAR will email an RMA number and return instructions within [INSERT — typically 1–2 business days]. The RMA number must be visible on the outside of the returning package.
  3. Package the item. Repackage in original carton with all original packaging materials, accessories, and documentation. Include a copy of the order confirmation or RMA email inside the box.
  4. Ship via tracked carrier. Use a carrier that provides tracking and (for high-value items) insurance. SAR is not responsible for items lost or damaged in return shipping if not properly tracked/insured.
  5. Wait for inspection. SAR will inspect the returned item upon receipt and process your refund within the timeframe stated below.

9. Refund Processing

Once your return is received and inspected, SAR will:

  • Email you confirming receipt and inspection result within 2–3 business days
  • Process the refund to the original payment method
  • Allow 5–10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement (timing depends on your card issuer / bank)

Refund amount = item price minus applicable restocking fee and any return shipping deducted.

10. Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged from shipping:

  1. Inspect at delivery. Note any visible damage on the carrier's delivery receipt before signing. This is critical for damage claims.
  2. Photo-document. Take clear photos of the damaged packaging, the damaged item, and the shipping label.
  3. Contact SAR within 48 hours of delivery at [INSERT EMAIL] with order number, photos, and delivery details.
  4. SAR will work with the carrier and manufacturer to arrange replacement or refund. No restocking fee applies; SAR covers return shipping.

For defects discovered after the item is in use, see our Warranty Information page — that's the right path for product-failure issues.

11. Wrong Item Received

If SAR ships the wrong product:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery
  • SAR arranges return shipping at our cost
  • The correct item ships at our cost
  • No restocking fee

12. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before shipment at no cost.

Once an order has shipped, it falls under the standard Return Policy (including return shipping costs and any applicable restocking fee).

To cancel an order, contact us as quickly as possible at [INSERT EMAIL] or call 856.547.8700. Custom-configured and special-order items may not be cancellable once the order is in production — those are addressed in Section 4 above.

13. Exchanges

SAR does not currently process direct exchanges. To exchange an item:

  1. Return the original item per this Return Policy
  2. Place a new order for the replacement item
  3. Refund for the returned item processes when the return is inspected
