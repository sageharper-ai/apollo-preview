Schema is a small block of code that describes your business in a format search engines and AI assistants can read directly. It doesn't change how your pages look. It gives them a clear, consistent picture of who you are, what you offer and where you work.

There's one block for your home page and one or two for each of your four main service pages. The service page blocks include the questions and answers already shown on each page, so AI assistants can read them. Each card shows which page it belongs on and where to paste it in Wix. Wix already adds a short automatic business listing to your home page. The home page block is a fuller version with the same details.
