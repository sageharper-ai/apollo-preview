Schema Package: ProSite Property Inspections

prositepropertyinspections.com: five ready-to-paste code blocks for your website
Prepared: September 25, 2026Prepared for: Blair Ginsberg5 schema blocks

Schema is a small block of code that describes your business in a format search engines and AI assistants can read directly. It doesn't change how your pages look. It gives them a clear, consistent picture of who you are, what you offer and where you work.

There's one block for your home page and one for each of your four main service pages. Each card shows which page it belongs on and where to paste it in Wix. Wix already adds a short automatic business listing to your home page. The home page block is a fuller version with the same details. Please check the short list under each block and let us know if anything needs to change.

Your schema blocks: one per page, copy and paste

After you paste
  • Paste each block only on the page named on its card. Don't paste the same block on two pages.

  • If any detail changes later, such as your phone number, hours or service area, update the installed markup in Wix and the visible page details, then republish.
