4. Excluded Damages and Conditions

The SAR Workmanship Warranty and (where applicable) manufacturer warranties do not cover:

  • Equipment installed by a third party not authorized by SAR or the manufacturer
  • Damage from misuse, modification, unauthorized repair, or improper operation
  • Damage from accidents, vandalism, or acts of nature
  • Normal wear-and-tear consumable items (filters, hoses, seals, belts, fluids beyond manufacturer terms)
  • Damage from operating the equipment outside its rated specifications (pressure, voltage, temperature, fluid type)
  • Damage caused by power surges, voltage fluctuations, or improper electrical connection
  • Cosmetic damage that does not affect equipment function
  • Equipment used for purposes other than those for which it was designed

9. Returns vs. Warranty

If you received the wrong product, or your product arrived damaged in shipping, that's a return, not a warranty claim. See our Return Policy for those situations.

The distinction: returns apply when the item never worked as expected from delivery or the wrong item arrived; warranty applies when the item worked and then failed within the warranty period.
