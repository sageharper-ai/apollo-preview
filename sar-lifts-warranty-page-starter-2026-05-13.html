Our Warranty Promise

When you purchase equipment from ShopAutoEquip.com, you're protected by two layers of coverage: the manufacturer's warranty on the product itself, and SAR Automotive Equipment's commitment to making sure your equipment performs as it should.

We work directly with each manufacturer's warranty team to streamline claims on your behalf. You don't have to chase the manufacturer — start your claim with us and we'll handle the coordination.

SAR Automotive Equipment Workmanship Warranty

In addition to manufacturer coverage, SAR Automotive Equipment warrants its installation and service work for [INSERT PERIOD — e.g., 90 days / 6 months / 1 year] from the date of installation or service.

This SAR Workmanship Warranty covers:

  • Installation defects — equipment installed by SAR that fails due to installation error
  • Service work — repairs and service performed by SAR or SAR-authorized technicians
  • Components SAR supplies — replacement parts and accessories sold and installed by SAR

This warranty does not cover:

  • Equipment installed by a third party
  • Damage from misuse, modification, or improper operation
  • Normal wear-and-tear consumable items (filters, hoses, seals beyond manufacturer terms)
  • Damage from environmental conditions outside the equipment's rated operating range
