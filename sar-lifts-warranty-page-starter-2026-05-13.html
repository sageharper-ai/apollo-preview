In addition to manufacturer coverage, SAR Automotive Equipment warrants its installation and service work for [INSERT PERIOD — e.g., 90 days / 6 months / 1 year] from the date of installation or service.

This SAR Workmanship Warranty covers:

  • Installation defects — equipment installed by SAR that fails due to installation error
  • Service work — repairs and service performed by SAR or SAR-authorized technicians

This warranty does not cover:

  • Equipment installed by a third party
  • Damage from misuse, modification, or improper operation
  • Normal wear-and-tear consumable items (filters, hoses, seals beyond manufacturer terms)
