Griffin Networks: website changes to approve

Step one of your search visibility work · 26 exact text changes across 9 pages · about 10 minutes

Prepared: October 12, 2026

Start here

What you are approving

These are the first changes to griffinnet.com. Each one is a specific piece of text, shown exactly as it reads today and exactly as it will read afterward. Nothing is left to interpretation: if it is not written on these pages, it is not changing in this step.

There are three kinds of text, and each page has one of each:

Search titleThe blue headline people see in Google, and the text on the browser tab.
Search descriptionThe two lines under that headline in Google. Eight of your nine pages do not supply one today, so Google writes its own.
Page headlineThe main headline at the top of the page itself. Your Home page does not have one marked as the main headline today.
Why these first. They tell Google and visitors what each page is about and where you work. Today most titles are a single word such as “Cabling”. The new ones name the service and Frederick, MD.

What is not changing in this step

  • No body text, images, layout, colors, menus or links.
  • No phone numbers, addresses or office details.
  • No new pages and no pages removed.
  • Your Privacy Policy text stays exactly as it is.

How to use this walkthrough

  1. Go through the nine pages. For each, choose Approve or Change.
  2. If you choose Change, type the wording you want. That is all we need.
  3. On the last step, add your name and send your decisions. It takes one click.

Last step

Review and send

What you are doing on this page
  1. Check your decisions. The box below lists each of the nine pages with what you chose: approved, change requested, or not decided.
  2. Add your name so HighRock knows who approved.
  3. Send them to HighRock. Press “Email my decisions”. A new message opens in your own email program with everything already written. Type your HighRock contact’s address in the To line and press send.

What happens next: nothing on your website changes until HighRock receives your email. Approved pages are then updated exactly as written here. For any page where you asked for a change, HighRock replies with the revised wording for you to approve before it goes live.
