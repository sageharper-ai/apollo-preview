5.The one thing we're fixing next

The most important upgrade isn't more budget — it's trust in the numbers. Right now Google can see when someone reaches your booking page, but not when they actually finish booking inside your Momence scheduler. So the bookings counted in this report reflect Google's best estimate of booking intent, not a Momence-confirmed headcount.

We're wiring Google directly to Momence (Apollo + Brook) so the numbers become exact — every real sign-up counted, nothing over- or under-stated. This is the single biggest upgrade to everything downstream: once it's live, the budget can open to the full $50/day with every extra dollar chasing confirmed clients, not doorway traffic.

Bottom line: The trend is real and it's up — May to July, bookings more than doubled. The Momence connection is what turns "up and to the right" into a number you can take to the bank.
