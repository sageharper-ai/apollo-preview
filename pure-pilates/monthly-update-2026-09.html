This update at a glance

Your ads keep reaching more people. Impressions grew again to 8,223 in September, more than double July's 3,661, and clicks are up 44% on July (338 vs 234), with spend steady at about $25 a day.

The challenge is what happens after the click: fewer clicks are turning into bookings. In July about 1 in 16 clicks became a booking; in August 1 in 31; in September 1 in 85. We checked the path on October 2 and it works end to end: the ad's landing page, the sign-up form and the booking page all load, and bookings are being counted. The slide started before September; the website attack made September worse (from September 19 the site was throttled for about three days and a protective wait screen ran through September 30).

The honest number: 4 tracked bookings in September (12 in August, 15 in July), so cost per booking rose to $190.76. With the booking path confirmed working, the question is why fewer clicks choose to book.

2. What we did in September

  • Diagnosed the website flood and planned the fix with your web team (Sept 19-22). We traced the traffic, built the protective setup checklist and worked it through with My Clone Solution until your host lifted its restriction on September 22.
  • Appealed the flagged ads (Sept 22). Google had flagged all 15 ads because the page wouldn't load during the attack; we appealed as soon as the site answered normally again.
  • Turned off the wait screen and set an attack alert (Sept 30). Visitors now reach the site directly. Two protective rules stay on, and an automatic alert now flags any new flood.
  • Kept the August fixes in place. The eight out-of-area town blocks from August are still live.

Where the budget converts: The "near me" searches were still where bookings came from: early September (Sept 1–7), before the site trouble, produced 2 of the month's 4 tracked bookings.

3. The one thing we're doing next

Compare July's and September's clicks search by search to find what changed in who is clicking, and shift the budget back toward the searches that book.

Also planned: the separate "near me" campaign test is on hold for now.
