This update at a glance

August was your most visible month yet: your ads appeared in 28% of the searches they were eligible for (June was 14%), impressions nearly doubled July's, and 370 people clicked through — the most in any month so far.

The honest number: 12 tracked bookings against $760 of spend puts August's cost per booking at $63 — higher than July's $40. Part of the story is a leak we found and fixed mid-month: about one dollar in five was going to searches for towns you don't serve — people nearby searching for Morristown, Westfield, Newark and five others. Your location targeting was set correctly from day one; it controls where the searcher is, not what they type, so this is the layer it can't catch. Your own question about "pilates near me" on August 24 kicked off that audit. We blocked all eight towns on August 25.

Early read since the fix: zero dollars have gone to those towns, and "pilates near me" — your best-converting search — is now the single biggest spend line. The full before-and-after lands next week, as promised.

2. What we did in August

  • Blocked eight out-of-area town searches (Aug 25). Searches for Morristown, Westfield, Newark, Florham Park, Springfield, Basking Ridge, Livingston, and Cranford had taken roughly $98 over the prior 30 days without producing a single booking. All eight are now negative keywords — added to the campaign's list (264 → 272 terms) and verified live in Google Ads.
  • Protected your service area while doing it. Summit, New Providence, Berkeley Heights, Chatham, Short Hills, and Millburn are untouched. We deliberately did not block "union," "elizabeth," or "warren" — those words are ambiguous (Summit sits in Union County) and blocking them could cost real local searches.
  • Answered your Aug 24 question with data. You noticed "pilates near me" converts best on the least money — confirmed: over the 30 days ending Aug 25, the near-me family produced 8 of 9 bookings on 49% of spend, and "pilates near me" itself booked at $35.86 each.

Where the budget converts: the near-me searches. Since the town fix (Aug 26 – Sep 2): zero dollars to the blocked towns, and "pilates near me" is now the top spend line — exactly where the bookings come from.

Early read on 8 days of data; the full before-and-after comes next week.

3. The one thing we're doing next

We ran the town-fix before-and-after, and it answers your separate-campaign question: even with the town waste gone, the campaign still loses roughly a quarter of its eligible impressions to the budget cap — and "pilates near me," your best booker, shares one budget with everything else. Our proposed test, using only your existing $25/day: split it into a dedicated "near me" campaign at $15/day and $10/day for everything else (brand searches, service terms, local towns). No new spend. We run it for a month, then show you the numbers. Nothing changes until you say go — and there is no rush on this.
