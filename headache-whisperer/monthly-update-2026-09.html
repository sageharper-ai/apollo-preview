This update at a glance

The ads are reaching more people every month: 38,150 impressions in September (14,624 in July) and 1,471 landing page visits, nearly double August's 746 and more than four times July's 336, at about 34 cents each, the lowest cost per visit so far.

The problem is what happens on the registration page: those 1,471 visits produced 3 tracked registrations. We checked the tracking on October 2 and it works on both registration pages, so this is a sign-up problem, not a counting problem.

The honest number: 3 tracked registrations from 1,471 visits in September (about 1 in 490). Turning visits into sign-ups is the priority.

2. What we did in September

  • Switched all ads to the live September 25 session (Sept 14). All eight ads were pointed to the live registration page, and the live-session ads ran on their own $25/day budget.
  • Kept the on-demand ads ready but paused. The evergreen ads now point to the on-demand page and stay paused while a live session is being promoted.
  • Paused after the session and restarted for October (Sept 25 and 30). The ads paused when the September session ended and came back on September 30, once the October session dates were on the calendar.

Where the budget converts: The live-session ads brought in 934 of September's 1,471 landing page visits, at about 34 cents each.
