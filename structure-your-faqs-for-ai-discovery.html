What AI is actually looking for

01

Structured Authority

Does your website clearly answer specific questions? FAQ pages, service pages with depth, location-specific content.

02

Trust Signals

E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. Reviews, credentials, consistent business info across the web.

03

Topical Depth

AI wants comprehensive coverage of your expertise. 20 related questions answered well beats one thin "Services" page.

Three ways to make your FAQs AI-friendly

No special vocabulary. No technical setup. Just how you already talk to a customer.

1

Write like people talk

No one searches "Pricing" — they search "how much does this cost?"

Use the question your customer actually asks on the phone.

2

Give a real answer

"Yes, call us" is not an answer.

Tell them when, how much, where. One concrete thing AI can repeat.

3

Say where you are

Local search needs a local answer.

Put your city in the answer, not buried in the footer.

Same question. Two answers. Watch what changes.

Q: Do you take walk-ins?

Before

Yes, walk-ins welcome. Please call ahead if possible.

11 words. Nothing here AI can repeat to a customer.

After

Yes — our Charlotte location accepts walk-ins Monday through Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM, and Saturdays until 2 PM. Sundays are appointment-only. To guarantee a time, we recommend booking online the day before.

Now AI knows your hours, where you are, and how to send people to you.

Three prompts to find your right 15 questions

01

Mine "People Also Ask"

"Search Google for [my main service] in [my city]. Extract every question from the 'People Also Ask' box. Group them by intent: pricing, scheduling, qualifications, what-to-expect."

02

Mine your inbox

"Here are 20 customer emails from the last 6 months [paste]. Pull every question they asked before buying. Cluster them. Tell me which 10 came up more than once."

03

Mine your competitors

"Visit [3 competitor URLs]. List every FAQ they answer. Mark which ones I should answer differently or better. Flag the questions none of them answer that I should own."

Run all three. You'll have your 15 questions in 30 minutes. Then you write the answers.
