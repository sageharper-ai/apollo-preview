The short version

Google is showing your site a lot — 36,748 times in the last 90 days — but mostly on page two, where almost nobody clicks. That produced 64 visits.

Your map listing is a different story, and a much better one. On the searches that matter locally, your Google Business Profile ranks between 2nd and 7th while your website ranks 4th to 28th. Most of your visibility is already coming from the map, not the site.

The website is also moving: several of your most valuable local searches climbed sharply this quarter, some by more than 40 positions.

The thing that converts page-two visibility into page-one traffic is published content — and thirteen articles are now live on your site. Ten cover the searches your customers actually type. Three are your own project stories, written from our May interview.

The one thing still missing is photographs of your work. That's the difference between a story that reads well and a story that proves something.

⚠ Outside Frederick County, you don't appear at all

We track the same searches from Rockville and Clarksburg. Every single one comes back not ranking — not low, not page three. Absent.

That's worth a decision rather than a fix. Montgomery County is a real market and you do work there, but competing in it is a separate effort from consolidating Frederick. It also confirms what you said in May about Clarksburg — there's nothing there for you, and the data agrees. My recommendation is to keep the focus on Frederick where you're already close, and revisit Montgomery deliberately if you want it.

The honest assessment

The technical foundation is doing its job. 959 ranking searches and 36,748 impressions is not a site Google is ignoring — it's a site Google is testing, and this quarter it moved several of your most commercially valuable local searches up by double digits.

What it lacked was depth on the topics you want to own — and page-two positions do not become page-one positions on their own. Thirteen articles now address that directly.

Be realistic about the timeline: new content does not move rankings in a week. Google has to find these pages, index them, and decide where they belong. Expect meaningful movement over the next 60–90 days, and judge it against the same four numbers at the top of this page — not against anything re-framed later.

The thing I'd put your attention on is the three story pieces. Ten search articles help Google understand what you do. The stories are the only content on your site that nobody else in Frederick County could publish, because they're your jobs. You have thirty years of them. We've written three.
