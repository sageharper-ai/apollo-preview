Five high-intent query patterns tested across AI surfaces and Google AI Overviews. Findings:

EBN absent. AI surfaces cite BNI, Alignable, EO, RemotelyFounders, and SCORE.

Your category is "curated boutique networking" — none of the cited competitors match it. Schema establishes the category position.

Query: alternative business networking groups to BNI for creative entrepreneurs

EBN absent. AI surfaces cite Meetup, LinkedIn Groups, Zerply, 4Networking, EO, Chamber, AMA, and accountants-niche groups.

This is your strongest competitive query (you actively differentiate from BNI's quota model). Schema gives AI surfaces something to attribute when this question gets asked.

Query: monthly virtual networking events with curated breakout rooms

AI surfaces cite platform tools — Remo, GlueUp, Webex, Airmeet, RingCentral — not community brands.

The category — "curated community with breakout-room events" — has no established named-competitor surface yet. Schema can help establish EBN's category position.

Query: online networking communities with intentional partner matching for women entrepreneurs

EBN absent. AI surfaces cite Dreamers & Doers, SheWorx, BossBabe Société, Entreprenista, and Women Who Startup.

EBN serves a related but distinct audience — your filter is creative service-based entrepreneurship rather than gender-organized community. Schema clarifies the entity for AI surfaces.

Branded query: Empowered Business Networking Stephanie Bonte-Lebair

EBN-owned surfaces appear (your site, Authority Magazine interview, your LinkedIn). No misattribution.

The branded query baseline is clean. The deploy work is about extending visibility beyond name-recognition into category-relevant queries.

6. Entity Chain Architecture summary

The entity graph connects four layers:

  • Layer 1 — Brand & Person Organization (EBN) ↔ Person (Stephanie) with founder relationship. Reinforced by Authority Magazine mention and member testimonials. Stephanie's credentials anchor the authority claim.
  • Layer 2 — Service & Events Service (Membership) ↔ Event × 2 (Networking Experience + Implementation Roundtable as subEvent). Stephanie as performer/organizer threads through. This is the most distinctive part of the graph — most networking communities don't expose recurring events as schema.
  • Layer 3 — Pages WebPage + BreadcrumbList establish the canonical URL and minimal hierarchy. Single-page-site architecture means the schema density per page is unusually high — to your benefit.
  • Layer 4 — Content FAQPage (50 Q&As) makes each question independently citable by AI surfaces. Reviews + AggregateRating provide the trust-signal layer.

Full graph diagram and entity-by-entity detail are in the companion Entity Chain Architecture document delivered with this report.
