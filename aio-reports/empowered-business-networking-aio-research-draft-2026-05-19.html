Q. How is EBN different from BNI?

A. BNI runs on a strict referral-quota model with weekly attendance, single-business-category-per-chapter exclusivity, and a transactional referral-tracking culture. EBN was built specifically for the creative service-based entrepreneur category — overlap is welcome (and often productive), and the curation accounts for it.
