What's next

  1. Break the one-page site into real pages.Publish your FAQ groups as their own pages — membership, events, how breakouts get curated — plus a standing page for the two monthly events. Each new page is a new thing AI can cite. This is the single biggest structural fix, and it is what closes the Gemini gap: Gemini knows who you are, it just has no specific page to point at.
  2. Get into the sources AI already reads.When we tested, the assistants pulled repeatedly from the same handful of places — SCORE, the US Chamber's CO-, Meetup, LinkedIn and the "best networking groups" roundups. Getting EBN listed in those is what wins the broad questions like "alternatives to BNI." Structured data cannot; placement can. Start with a SCORE profile and one roundup pitch.
  3. Say your full name everywhere.In bylines, guest appearances, podcast intros and directory listings, use "Empowered Business Networking," not "EBN." A similarly named site, ebnetworking.com, is currently collecting credit on answers that describe your business — consistent full-name usage is what separates you from it.
