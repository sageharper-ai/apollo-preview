Pricing & Scope

Q1.How much does done-for-you social media management cost?Apollo-drafted

Done-for-you social media management typically ranges from $500–$10,000+ per month depending on scope, platforms, and strategy depth. At Oh Snap! Social, our Corporate Done-For-You tier begins at $750/month for full-service strategy, content creation, scheduling, and community management. We also offer one-time intensives like Brainstorm in a Snap! ($950) and Content in a Snap! ($2,200) for businesses that want focused output without an ongoing monthly commitment.

Q2.What's included in a done-for-you social media service?Apollo-drafted

A complete done-for-you service includes strategy development, custom content creation (graphics + copy + video), posting and scheduling, community management, and performance analytics. Oh Snap! Social's Corporate Done-For-You tier wraps all of this into a four-phase engagement: Audit → Strategy → Implementation (3-5 posts per week with custom images) → Outreach Management. We handle the work; you stay focused on running your business.

Q3.What's the typical 6-month outcome for Corporate Done-For-You clients?Your founder voice

There is no typical outcome, just your outcome and the goals you have for your social media efforts! Everything we do for our corporate and small business clients is customized to them and what they're looking to get out of social media. Sure, we have a framework to help with the path forward, but as for the strategy and content creation that's completely custom to the goals of our clients.

Q4.What's the difference between done-for-you and done-with-you social media?Apollo-drafted

Done-for-you (DFY) means we handle everything end-to-end — strategy, content, posting, and engagement. Done-with-you (DWY) means we build the strategy and the foundational assets with you, then you (or your team) execute against the plan. Oh Snap! Social offers both: our Corporate tier is DFY for service businesses with full delegation needs; our Solopreneurs & Tiny Teams option is DWY for owners who want hands-on involvement with strategic support.

Q5.Do I have to provide content or do you create everything?Apollo-drafted

Oh Snap! Social handles content creation — we write the copy, design the graphics, and produce the social-ready assets. We do ask you to provide source material we can't generate ourselves: brand voice samples, product photos, customer stories, and access to anything proprietary. That collaboration in the first 30 days produces a content calendar template we use throughout the engagement.

Service Specifics

Q6.What platforms does Oh Snap! Social manage?Apollo-drafted

Oh Snap! Social manages social media across the platforms where your audience actually lives — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, and X. We don't believe in posting everywhere; the Pulse Check phase identifies the 2-3 platforms with the highest signal-to-noise ratio for your business and we focus the strategy there. Spreading thin across every channel is one of the most expensive mistakes service businesses make.

Q7.How many posts per week does Oh Snap! Social create?Apollo-drafted

Our Corporate Done-For-You tier produces 3-5 posts per week on identified social channels, with custom images and links designed to convert followers into lifelong fans. Cadence is set during the Strategize phase based on your platform mix and audience behavior, not on a one-size formula.

Q8.Does Oh Snap! Social handle paid advertising?Your edit applied

Yes. Several Oh Snap! Social engagements include paid social management alongside organic content. Karlyn and the team approach paid social as an accelerant for organic strategy — not a replacement. Shaw Services, a long-time Oh Snap! Social client, reports that consistent call-volume increases followed once we layered paid into their existing program — "she's timely, she's hip, she doesn't oversell our clients with pushy ads. Our call volume has increased consistently since we partnered with her."

Q9.What is the Fan Firestarter Framework?Your edit applied

The Fan Firestarter Framework is Karlyn Ankrom's proprietary approach to social media marketing built for businesses ready to Strategize (make a plan for progress) instead of approaching content creation reactively. It moves through three phases — Pulse Check (current state), Strategize (optimization), Advance (implementation) — and emphasizes converting passive followers into active fans through consistent, opinionated content rather than algorithmic guesswork.

Q10.What are the phases of working with Oh Snap! Social?Apollo-drafted

Every engagement walks the Fan Firestarter Framework: Pulse Check audits where you are; Strategize designs the optimization plan; Advance turns the plan into a content calendar and execution rhythm. Corporate-tier clients add two more phases — Strategy detailed planning and Implementation + Acceleration with 3-5 weekly posts plus Outreach Management for community engagement.

Q11.What inspired the Fan Firestarter Framework?Your founder voice

The Fan Firestarter Framework is our signature framework that is platform and algorithm agnostic. It helps our clients and us see the path from where we are currently (The Pulse Check) all the way through where we want to go (The Strategy) before we ever create a single piece of content that helps us Advance and get closer to the goals and successes we're looking for collectively — the raving and paying fans we're craving!

Process

Q16.How does the Pulse Check phase work?Apollo-drafted

Pulse Check is the audit phase. We look at your current social media footprint across every platform you're active on, evaluate the gap between where you are and where your strategy should be, and surface the highest-leverage moves. Output: a clear written assessment plus the foundation for the Strategize phase. Pulse Check is also available as a standalone audit (Snap Scan) for businesses that want diagnostic insight without a full engagement.

Q17.What happens during the Strategize phase?Apollo-drafted

Strategize translates the Pulse Check findings into a concrete optimization plan: which platforms get attention, what content pillars structure the calendar, what tone and voice carry the brand, what the posting cadence looks like, and what specific outcomes the next 90 days are organized around. The Strategize output is your custom 6-month editorial calendar plus Ideal Client Profile.

Q18.What does Advance and Outreach Management look like?Apollo-drafted

Advance is execution — the content calendar going live, posts shipping on schedule, the brand voice landing consistently. Outreach Management (Corporate-tier addition) layers in community engagement: responding to comments, building relationships in the comments and DMs of relevant accounts, and turning passive followers into engaged community members.

Q19.What happens at the end of a Corporate engagement?Your founder voice

I hate it when agencies take all the collateral that you ultimately paid for and run. That's the most unprofessional way to do business. From our connections on Meta — where you invite us to your business portfolios — to walking away with a complete offboarding packet that includes downloadable links to your content calendar, asset libraries of the creative we've built. It's all yours: the data, the creative, the archive of content on your Google sheet-style calendar. It's yours.

Q20.Does Oh Snap! Social provide analytics reports?Apollo-drafted

Yes. Monthly reporting captures the metrics from the Strategize phase, tracks progress against the 90-day goals, and surfaces what's working / what needs adjusting. We use the Metrics That Matter framework so you see signal, not vanity. Quarterly deep-dives review the bigger trends and inform the next phase of the engagement.

Solopreneurs & Tiny Teams is built for owner-operators who want strategic support and content frameworks but prefer to keep execution hands-on (DWY rather than DFY).

It's the lightest-touch way to work with us and a common starting point before deciding on a larger engagement. Pricing is available on application — contact the team for current rates.

Program Basics

Q26.What is the Expert Excellence Engine?Apollo-drafted

The Expert Excellence Engine (EEE) is Oh Snap! Social's six-month group program for heart-centered, brainiac authors who already have a published book and want to turn that work into more visibility, influence, and results. Karlyn Ankrom hosts and facilitates. The program combines Weekly Ask Me Anything sessions, monthly implementation hours, quarterly AI challenges, and personalized onboarding into a structured arc you can walk alongside other published authors.

Q27.Who is the Expert Excellence Engine for?Apollo-drafted

EEE is for big-hearted brainiacs — published nonfiction authors with their book already out in the world who want to translate their expertise into a thriving online presence. The "already published" qualifier matters: EEE is built around having an asset to amplify, not around the book-writing process itself.

Q28.How long does the Expert Excellence Engine program run?Apollo-drafted

EEE is a six-month commitment. The arc is designed deliberately — six months gives you enough time to walk the editorial calendar across a full quarter, run a quarterly AI challenge, and develop the rhythm that makes social presence sustainable past the program.

Q29.What's the difference between EEE and the Oh Snap! Social DFY agency?Apollo-drafted

EEE is done-WITH-you in a group setting — Karlyn teaches, you implement, you stay in the driver's seat. The DFY agency is done-FOR-you — Oh Snap! Social handles everything. EEE is built for authors who want to learn and run their own social presence as part of building their brand; the DFY agency is for business owners who want to delegate execution.

Q30.What is the Founding-Member rate?Apollo-drafted

EEE is currently open at a Founding-Member rate — a launch pricing tier reserved for the first cohort. Two payment options are available: $3,000 full payment, or a 4-payment plan of $825 per installment ($3,300 total). The rate you join at is the rate you stay at; this Founding-Member pricing is reserved for the first cohort and will not be repeated.

Deliverables

Q31.What are Weekly Ask Me Anything Opportunities?Apollo-drafted

Every week of EEE includes a live Ask Me Anything window where Karlyn answers your specific questions about content, strategy, platforms, audience, or anything else surfacing in your work. AMAs are how the program adapts to your real-time challenges rather than running on a fixed curriculum that may not match where you are.

Q32.What happens in the Private Onboarding Call?Apollo-drafted

Your onboarding call is a one-on-one with Karlyn focused on building your custom six-month editorial calendar and clarifying your Ideal Client Profile. This is where the program gets personalized — the editorial calendar that comes out of this call becomes the spine of your work for the next six months.

Q33.What are Monthly Implementation Power Hours?Apollo-drafted

Power Hours are monthly working sessions where you implement what you've been learning in real time, alongside other EEE members, with Karlyn available for live troubleshooting. The goal is to turn the program from theoretical to lived — content gets made, calendars get filled, posts go live during the hour.

Q34.What are Monthly Snappy Session Trainings?Apollo-drafted

Snappy Session Trainings are monthly focused teaching modules — short, sharp, single-topic deep-dives on the specific social media skills authors most need. Subjects rotate based on cohort feedback and what's moving in the platforms; recent examples include content repurposing, AI prompt engineering for social, and platform-algorithm shifts.

Q35.What are Quarterly AI Challenges?Apollo-drafted

Each quarter, EEE runs a focused AI challenge — a structured 1-2 week sprint where members experiment with specific AI tools and prompt frameworks to generate, repurpose, or accelerate content. Karlyn provides the structure; members share what they tried and what worked. Output: a working playbook each author leaves the quarter with.

Author-Specific Application

Q36.Can EEE help with my book launch on social media?Apollo-drafted

Yes. Many EEE members are using the program to support an active or upcoming book launch — Karlyn's onboarding call structures the six-month editorial calendar around your specific milestones. Whether you're pre-launch, in the launch window, or working long-tail visibility post-launch, the calendar adapts.

Q37.How does EEE differ from a regular social media agency for authors?Apollo-drafted

A regular agency handles execution on your behalf — you provide brand input and they post. EEE teaches you the strategy and supports you while you execute, so the capability lives with you. For nonfiction authors specifically, that ownership matters: your social presence becomes an extension of your expertise, not a contracted service line that disappears when the contract ends.

Q38.Do I need to already have a book published to join EEE?Apollo-drafted

Yes. EEE is built around already having a published book "out in the wild" to amplify. The program's frameworks for editorial calendar, content repurposing, and authority positioning all assume an existing book asset. If you're pre-publication, Oh Snap! Social's other consulting offerings may be a better fit until your book is out.

Q39.How does EEE help authors build their platform?Apollo-drafted

Platform building in EEE happens through the editorial calendar (consistent presence), the Fan Firestarter Framework (audience-to-fan progression), and the community surface (peer authors who become collaborators and cross-promoters). Six months of structured presence builds a platform that compounds rather than spiking and fading.

Q40.Can EEE help me find my ideal reader/client?Apollo-drafted

Yes — Ideal Client Profile work is part of the onboarding call and runs through the entire program. The Strategize phase of the Fan Firestarter Framework specifically focuses on identifying who you're for, what they need, and how your content speaks to them. EEE members report increased clarity on reader fit by month two or three.
