1. Executive Summary

Families in Hillsborough County search for memory care in plain words: "memory care" is searched 2,200 times a month and "dementia care near me" another 2,200, while "memory care Tampa Palms", the phrase your site leans on, has no volume record at all. You are well regarded (4.8 stars from 22 Google reviews) and your referring-domain count is close to every competitor we measured, yet you rank for the fewest keywords in the group (62). The one thing that matters most is dedicated pages: there is no dedicated service page for dementia and Alzheimer's care, for the respite stays you offer, or for families working out how to pay, and those three topics carry 5,475 monthly searches in the county between them.

Correct the phone number and add hours on your Google Business Profile

Write meta descriptions for your core pages

Retitle the homepage and the reviews page around what families search

Dementia and Alzheimer's care, respite stays, and paying for care (Medicaid waiver and VA). See the content gap table in section 3 for the demand behind each.

Add local business structured data

Speed up the mobile homepage
