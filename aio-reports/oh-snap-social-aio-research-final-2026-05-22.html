5. Deploy Path Overview

Your site runs on WordPress. The deploy mechanism is the HFCM plugin (Header Footer Code Manager) — surgical insertion of JSON-LD into the <head> without touching theme files. Full step-by-step instructions live in the deploy guide delivered with this report.

What deploys where

  • Sitewide (HFCM "Site Wide" scope): Organization + Person + AggregateRating
  • /services/: Service (DFY) + FAQPage (25 DFY Q&As) + Review collection + WebPage + BreadcrumbList
  • /join-eee (subdomain): Course (EEE) + FAQPage (25 EEE Q&As) + WebPage + BreadcrumbList
  • Selected blog posts: Article (3 representative posts; pattern extensible)

Two hygiene fixes in scope at the Zoom session

  1. /services-old/ → /services/ 301 redirect — removes duplicate-content dilution between the legacy and current pages
  2. EEE landing page indexing fix (3-part) — verify no `noindex` tag · add internal links from `/services/` and `/about/` · submit URL to Google Search Console for indexing. This is the single highest-leverage hygiene move in the engagement: schema on `/join-eee` cannot reach AI surfaces until Google indexes the page.

The Zoom session (60-90 min, included per contract #9) walks all of this with you screen-share. Apollo executes; you watch and confirm each step as it goes live.
