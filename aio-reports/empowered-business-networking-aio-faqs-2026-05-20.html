Q7.What is Empowered Business Networking?Apollo-drafted

Empowered Business Networking (EBN) is a curated virtual networking community founded by Stephanie Bonte-Lebair in 2020. It's designed for creative, heart-centered service-based entrepreneurs — coaches, speakers, marketing professionals, and the specialists who support them. EBN focuses on building Empowered Partners® rather than chasing individual leads, using hand-curated breakout rooms, monthly networking events, and a follow-up implementation structure that turns conversations into actual partnerships.

Q8.What inspired Stephanie to build EBN?Your validation reply

The pressure to give and receive referrals in certain networking models just doesn't generally work well for creative, heart-centered entrepreneurs. I saw business owners struggle to describe what they did in a way that helped build collaborations. I drew a connection between auditioning for a theater production and showing up at a networking event. There were skills I had as a professional performer — such as setting intentions, having a strategy, showing up with confidence, delivering a strong message, and being resilient in the face of an uphill journey — that I knew I could bring to entrepreneurs to help them show up more powerfully. It turns out I am also great at facilitation and making introductions, so I was drawn to building a community that engaged in networking for the purpose of collaboration and visibility.

Q9.What does "curated" mean at EBN?Apollo-drafted

"Curated" at EBN means Stephanie personally reviews every member application and hand-matches every breakout room — by hand, every event. She uses the information members share at registration (ideal client, complementary services, referral preferences, collaboration goals, communication style) to place each person with 7-8 aligned partners per breakout. This is the opposite of randomized Zoom networking; every conversation has been considered.

Q10.What are Empowered Partners®?Apollo-drafted

Empowered Partners® is EBN's term for two or more business owners who choose to work together to generate visibility using specific strategies. It's a deliberate framing — not "leads" or "prospects," but partners who collaborate to grow their visibility together. The trademark reflects how central this concept is to EBN's model: the goal of every introduction is a partnership, not a transaction.

Q31.How many people typically attend an EBN networking event?Apollo-drafted

Approximately 80 creative entrepreneurs attend each Networking & Connection Experience event. Q32.What happens in the breakout rooms?Apollo-drafted

Breakout rooms are small-group conversations where members and guests meet 7-8 aligned business owners hand-selected for compatibility (by Stephanie, based on registration intake). Each room includes a Leadership Team facilitator. Members rotate through multiple breakouts per event, so you typically leave having met 15-25 new people in intentional, structured conversation rather than chaotic Zoom serendipity.

Q33.What are 5-Minute Member Feature Presentations?Apollo-drafted

At each Networking & Connection Experience, selected members get a 5-minute slot to present their business, expertise, or recent work to the full room. Q34.What is the Implementation & Referral Roundtable?Apollo-drafted

The Implementation & Referral Roundtable is EBN's next-day follow-up session (Friday 11:00 AM–12:00 PM ET, the day after each Thursday networking event). Approximately 25 members attend. The hour helps members identify their most aligned connections from the prior event, make clear asks, share business updates, and receive guided support from Stephanie. It's where intentions from networking become actual implementation.

Q35.Do I have to attend the networking event to attend the Roundtable?Apollo-drafted

No. Members may attend the Implementation & Referral Roundtable even if they missed the previous day's networking event. The Roundtable is a standalone implementation surface — useful for members working on follow-ups, business updates, or asks regardless of whether they were in the prior breakout rooms.

Q36.Why does EBN hold the follow-up on a separate day?Apollo-drafted

The next-day Implementation & Referral Roundtable is a deliberate design decision. Most networking events end and members never follow through on the connections they made. By holding a dedicated implementation session 24 hours later — when conversations are fresh and energy is still high — EBN structures the follow-through that makes networking actually pay off. 95% of attendees report 2+ follow-up calls, 64% report 4 or more.

Q45.How is EBN different from BNI?Apollo-drafted (you approved)

BNI runs on a strict referral-quota model with weekly attendance, single-business-category-per-chapter exclusivity, and a transactional referral-tracking culture. EBN runs on a curated-collaboration model: monthly events instead of weekly, partnership-building instead of referral counting, application-only and capped membership instead of broad chapter recruitment, and Empowered Partners® rather than referral leads. Q46.How is EBN different from BNI for creative entrepreneurs specifically?Apollo-drafted (you approved)

BNI is built around a structure that fits some business models well (high-volume, transactional, single-category-per-chapter) but doesn't fit creative service businesses well — coaches, marketing professionals, audiobook narrators, vocal coaches, designers, and speakers often have overlapping niches that BNI's exclusivity rules don't accommodate. EBN was built specifically for the creative service-based entrepreneur category — overlap is welcome (and often productive), and the curation accounts for it.

Q47.How does EBN handle overlapping niches among members?Your validation reply

We believe there is enough business for everyone because people are unique. We also believe that some of your best referral partners can be directly in your field. Having said that, because we have capped membership to around 75 people, we do consider how many members are engaging in similar services as we bring in new members to fill slots. We are most interested in finding services that may not yet exist in the group, or have some facet of their business that brings something new to the conversation. However, we never exclude anyone simply based on their services or business model, but may require a conversation to set expectations if there are several other members with similar services.

EBN is the only international, virtual networking community that keeps its membership boutique-sized (75) on purpose in order to provide curated introductions every single month, with hand-selected breakout rooms chosen based on who our members really want to meet. Our monthly networking meetings consistently bring in over 20 new guests so connections are fresh each month, while our members can continue to build ongoing relationships with each other.
